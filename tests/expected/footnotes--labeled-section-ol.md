We laid out our position in an earlier essay[^1], and have held it consistently.

1. The first concern is that capable systems are hard to monitor once released[^2].
2. The second concern is that testing should apply to every sufficiently capable system[^3].

These positions are complementary rather than competing.

[^1]: See Sections 2 and 3 of that essay for the full discussion.

[^2]: See [this report](https://example.com/report) for supporting analysis.

[^3]: Testing regimes are described in *Appendix B* of the same document.
