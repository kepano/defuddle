## Short page

Red foxes are small, adaptable mammals found across much of the northern hemisphere. They live in forests, grasslands, mountains and even busy cities. Foxes are mostly active at dawn and dusk, when they hunt rodents, rabbits and birds. In autumn they also eat fruit, berries and insects to build up reserves before winter.
