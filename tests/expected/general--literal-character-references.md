A non-breaking space is written as **&amp;nbsp;** in HTML source.

To show a less than sign, write &amp;lt; or &amp;#60; or &amp;#x3C;.

| Result | Entity name | Entity number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \< | &amp;lt; | &amp;#60; |
| © | &amp;copy; | &amp;#169; |

A plain ampersand stays as it is, as in AT&T and a=1&b=2.
