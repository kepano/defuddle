The harbor wakes slowly. Fishing boats leave before dawn, gulls circle the pier, and the cafe on the corner opens its shutters at six. We spent a week there photographing the morning light.

Each morning brought a different sky, from heavy fog to clear gold, and the boats came back with the tide before noon.
