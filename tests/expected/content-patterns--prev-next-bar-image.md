The boats leave a little before sunrise, when the water inside the breakwater is still flat and the lights along the quay are still on. This photograph was taken from the end of the north pier.

![Fishing boats leaving the harbor at dawn](https://example.com/images/harbor-12.jpg)

The slow shutter turns the wake of each boat into a pale line across the harbor. The two boats on the left belong to the same family and have fished from this port for three generations.

Prints of this photograph are available in two sizes. The larger print shows the lighthouse on the far headland, which is cropped from the version shown on this page.
