## Section 1: Dismissal of claims

(1) This section applies where, in proceedings on a claim for damages in respect of personal injury, the court finds that the claimant is entitled to damages in respect of the claim. The court must consider all of the evidence submitted by the parties before reaching its decision on the application.

(2) The court must dismiss the primary claim, unless it is satisfied that the claimant would suffer substantial injustice if the claim were dismissed. In considering that question, the court must take account of the circumstances of the case and explain the reasons for its decision to each of the parties.

(3) An order under this section must record the amount of damages that the court would otherwise have awarded to the claimant. The order must also state how the court has calculated any deduction from those damages and identify the evidence on which that calculation is based. Each party must receive a copy of the order.

(4) In this section—

- “personal injury” includes any disease and any other impairment of a person's physical or mental condition;
- “related claim” means a claim for damages made by a person other than the person who made the primary claim.
