A home network connects your computers, phones and printers so they can share files and a single internet connection. Most of the work is done once, when the router is first configured.

Read the sections in order, because each step relies on the settings chosen in the one before it.

## Placing the router

Put the router near the middle of the home and away from thick walls and large metal objects. A high shelf usually gives better coverage than a spot on the floor.

## Securing the wireless network

Change the default administrator password, choose the strongest encryption the router offers, and give the network a name that does not reveal who lives there.

## Connecting your devices

Join each device to the new network and check that it can reach the internet. Printers and smart speakers often need their own setup app the first time.
