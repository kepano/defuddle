## Introduction

INTROCANARY The laboratory tray holds a measured layer of clean sand above a still water column. Each trial starts when a gate opens and lets the grains fall as a single pulse. Observers record the time until the water clears and the depth of the layer that remains on the tray floor. The setup uses the same tray, the same water depth, and the same release height for every trial so the comparison stays fair. A second series repeats the pulse after the sand has been rinsed and dried, which removes dust that would otherwise cloud the water.

The notes also list the room temperature, the mass poured in each pulse, and the number of trials kept after a spill. Nothing in this section restates the short card above. The purpose is to show how the grains move once the gate opens and before any tally is drawn.

## Results

RESULTSCANARY Across the kept trials the water cleared in a narrow band of times, and the layer on the tray floor grew in step with the poured mass. The rinsed sand left a thinner haze than the first series, so the floor mark was easier to read. A heavier pulse did not change the order of settling. Larger grains reached the floor first, and finer grains followed after the column calmed. The pattern held when the release height stayed fixed and only the poured mass changed.
