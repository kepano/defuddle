## Bread Basics

## Choosing flour

Bread flour has more protein than all-purpose flour, which gives the loaf a stronger structure and a chewier crumb. For a softer sandwich loaf, a mix of the two works well and is easier to shape by hand.

## Kneading the dough

Knead on a lightly floured surface for about ten minutes, folding the dough over itself and pressing it away with the heel of your hand. The dough is ready when it feels smooth and springs back slowly after a gentle poke.

## Baking and cooling

Bake in a hot oven until the crust is deep brown and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped underneath. Let it cool completely on a rack before slicing, or the inside will turn gummy as the steam escapes.
